About Us

A Little More Than Just a Place to Stay.

Apna Aangan PG aims to make finding accommodation in Dwarka, New Delhi, simpler and more comfortable. We bring property information, room options, pricing, amenities, and enquiries together to help residents make informed accommodation decisions.

Moving to a new city for a college seat, a first job, or a fresh start is exciting — and stressful. The hunt for a room usually means scattered broker calls, unclear pricing and surprise charges. We built Apna Aangan PG to fix exactly that: one place where Dwarka rooms are listed honestly, charges are written down before you visit, and a real person answers on call and WhatsApp.

What we stand for

🏡 Comfortable living

Furnished rooms that feel like a home, not a hostel bunk — with space to study, work and rest.

🧾 Transparent pricing

Rent, deposit, food, electricity and maintenance — itemised on every listing before you enquire.

💬 Helpful communication

Real answers on call and WhatsApp, free property visits, and written terms before any money moves.

🤝 Respectful community

Clear, fair house rules that protect everyone's comfort — students, professionals and parents alike.
