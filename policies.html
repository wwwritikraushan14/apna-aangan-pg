Our policies, in plain words

Note: These are editable template policies provided for review before launch. The business should have them reviewed and finalised with its actual terms before accepting bookings.

Privacy Policy

Apna Aangan PG ("we") collects only the information needed to respond to enquiries and manage bookings: your name, mobile number, optional email, accommodation preferences, and visit details submitted through this website or WhatsApp.

How we use it: to contact you about availability, schedule visits, maintain booking records, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing: your details are shared only with the property manager of the property you enquire about, and with service providers strictly as needed to operate this website.

Retention & your rights: enquiry records are kept for a reasonable period for business and legal purposes. You may ask us to correct or delete your information by contacting us on 8292160777, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

Security: this website uses HTTPS in production, server-side validation, and access-controlled administration. No method is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable safeguards.

Terms & Conditions

By using this website you agree to these terms. Listings describe accommodation offered in Dwarka, New Delhi; photographs and demonstration data are indicative until verified for a specific property.

Enquiries are not reservations. A room is reserved only after availability is confirmed personally and the booking is recorded with the applicable deposit, as confirmed in writing.

Resident obligations: residents must follow the property rules shown on their listing (visitors, notice period, minimum stay, and conduct), pay charges on time, and provide accurate identification documents before move-in.

Accuracy: we work to keep listing information accurate; if anything differs at the property, the written booking confirmation prevails.

Cancellation Policy

Visits: property visits are free and can be rescheduled or cancelled at any time by phone or WhatsApp.

Bookings: cancellation terms for a confirmed booking are stated in the written booking confirmation for that property. As a template default: cancellations made before move-in are eligible for a deposit review as per the confirmation; after move-in, the notice period stated on the listing applies.

To cancel, contact us on 8292160777 with your booking or enquiry reference.

Refund Policy

Security deposit: refundable deposits are returned after move-out, following inspection and adjustment of any genuine dues (unpaid rent, charges, or damage beyond normal wear), within the period stated on the property listing (commonly 15–21 days), to the payer's bank account.

Payments: this website does not collect card payments unless a verified payment gateway is configured; where online payments are enabled, refunds follow the gateway's timelines in addition to the above.

Disputes: contact us first on 8292160777 — we aim to resolve refund questions promptly and fairly.
